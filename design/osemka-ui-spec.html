The recording is the full exam soundtrack. At about 21 minutes for 3 transcript pages, the mp3 includes the instructions, both plays of each text, and the pauses. Keys have sheet versions. The math key lists “Rozwiązanie – wersja X” and “wersja Y”. The importer has to pin each attempt to one version, and the UI never shows a version letter to the student.
02 · Philosophy

Calm, honest, exam-shaped

The user is 14 or 15, often anxious, and preparing for a paper exam in a quiet room. The interface should feel like a good desk: tidy, well lit, nothing shouting.

One job per screen

Inside an attempt, the global nav disappears. The student sees the task, the answer area and one primary action. Everything else is one tap away, never on screen by default.

Two modes that look different

Practice uses the teal shell and feels open. Simulation switches to an indigo focus shell with a countdown. A student should never wonder which mode they are in.

Mistakes are information

Wrong answers use a muted brick color, never alarm red, and always come with “why” and the CKE rule. No streak shaming, no sad mascots, no loss animations.

Show the sample size

A trend from two tasks is noise. Every percentage in analytics carries n, and areas with fewer than 4 tasks say “za mało danych” instead of guessing.

Faithful to paper

Task numbers, line numbers, point ranges “(0–3)” and wording follow the arkusz exactly. The original PDF page is always reachable, so the student can trust the digital copy.

The student grades, AI advises

For open answers the student scores themselves against CKE criteria. AI suggestions appear in a separate box, are marked as suggestions and never overwrite the student's own score.

06 · Design system

Accessibility and accommodations

  • Contrast: body text 13:1 and muted text at least 4.5:1, in both themes.
  • Dark mode is designed, not inverted: lifted surfaces, desaturated subject colors, and reading text at 87 % luminance to avoid glare.
  • Keyboard: every attempt can be finished without a mouse. J/K next/previous, F flag, Enter check.
  • Wydłużony czas is a setting, not a per-attempt checkbox the student has to remember. It applies to simulations and shows in results (“125 → 185 min”).
  • Dysleksja: optional dyslexia-friendly font, and the CKE's separate spelling and punctuation thresholds for the essay (the key defines both scales).
  • Motion: respects reduced-motion. The only animation is a 150 ms fade between tasks.

  • Open math tasks get a typed work area with a small symbol bar, or “rozwiązałem na kartce” plus an optional photo. Both are graded later in Samoocena.
  • Browser fullscreen is offered on start but not forced.
